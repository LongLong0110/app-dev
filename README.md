# My Favorite Movie: Wicked

## About the Movie
*Wicked* is a musical fantasy movie about two young witches, Elphaba and Glinda, who meet at Shiz University and develop an unexpected friendship.

## My Favorite Characters
- **Elphaba** – A misunderstood witch with magical powers.
- **Glinda** – A popular and cheerful young witch.

## Why I Like This Movie
I like Wicked because of its beautiful songs, interesting story, and amazing characters. It teaches us not to judge people based on their appearance and to understand their differences.

## My Favorite Quote
> Everyone deserves a chance to be understood.

## Rating
**10/10** – A wonderful musical fantasy movie!
